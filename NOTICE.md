# Attribution and research basis

The blur-circle geometry and human-eye longitudinal chromatic aberration
constants in this project are based on the GPL-3.0 project:

- Refractify Myopic Defocus browser extension
- Copyright its respective contributors
- https://github.com/refractify/myopic_defocus

The visual approach is also informed by:

- Swiatczak B, Ingrassia L, Scholl HPN, Schaeffel F. “Pilot study:
  simulating myopic chromatic aberration on a computer screen induces
  progressive choroidal thickening in myopes.” Scandinavian Journal of
  Optometry and Visual Science, 17(2), 2024.
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.15626/sjovs.v17i2.4232

The study was a small pilot and does not establish this software as a medical
treatment. “Refractify” is a third-party name and is not used as this
application's product name.
